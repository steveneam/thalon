How this page was made

Fern & Crumb is a fictional demonstration business. This page is a portfolio template: it shows how a small café's landing page can be designed, written, and illustrated by an AI-driven content engine. Nothing on it is a real offer, address, or menu.

The design

The signature system is typographic. Headlines are set in Shantell Sans, a variable font with unusual axes — bounce and informality — and they proof like dough: each heading enters the viewport slightly flat and compressed, then swells to full weight over one breath. Without JavaScript, or with reduced motion enabled, the page is simply complete and fully risen. The ampersand in the wordmark is a drawn latte-fern rosette — the name's two halves joined by the pour — and it recurs as a small cup-stamp between chapters. The menu board, oven timeline, prices, and every other word on the page are HTML text, not images.

The imagery — all AI-generated

Every photograph on this page was generated with the Higgsfield Soul 2.0 model (six images, one take each). No stock photography, no scraped references. Two honest edits are disclosed: a small hand-written table card in the window-table photograph and a tiny lens artifact in the crumb photograph were locally blurred before archiving — the generator had invented an illegible scribble on the card, and we blur invented text rather than pretend it says something.

## Look-first taste notes (Dribbble café-branding · Dribbble café-landing · Pinterest morning-room register)

1. **The letterform borrows the food's physics.** The sweep's strongest work
   makes type edible: dough-soft rounded counters (MELO), a coffee bean seated
   inside the B's counter (Bean), a drip-shaped descender (DRIPDAY). The
   principle stolen (never the pixels): our display face must DO something
   food-physical, not just sit rounded — see the proofing mechanism below.
2. **Type lives on the café's own artifacts** — cups, kraft bags, awnings,
   boards, everywhere in the sweep. But for our pipeline every one of those is
   a proven livery magnet (soul invents lettering on them). Resolution: the
   artifact-letterform world is built ENTIRELY code-drawn (stamp marks, menu
   board, price tags as HTML/SVG), and every minted surface stays plain —
   the ⑯ lesson (all in-scene text code-drawn retires the pseudo-text class)
   carried over deliberately.
3. **The urban-nature register is the WINDOW.** The Pinterest room tiles agree:
   monstera and hanging ferns against big glass, the street tree seen THROUGH
   the pane, low raking morning sun, steam visible in backlight, checkerboard
   or timber grounding it. Light is the warmth layer; the frond SHADOW across
   a table is the house-style mirror move (nature echoing the latte fern).
4. **Drenched single-colour worlds read strong but are refused here** —
   DRIPDAY's all-green is the best landing in the sweep, but that pole belongs
   to ⑲'s Joji register. Fern & Crumb stays morning-parchment so the wave's
   three sites stay unmistakably distinct.
5. **Refused defaults (named, 12+ sightings):** the e-commerce product-card
   hero grid · "The Best Coffee In Your City" / "FREE LUXURY BAKERY" headline
   class · chalkboard-font menus · pastel doodle-illustration filler · the
   dark moody bakery hero with gold serif "premium" register · the Instagram
   strip section · beans-scattered-on-dark-table branding stock.

## The concept — TYPE THAT PROOFS LIKE DOUGH, in a room the morning fills

The signature system is typographic: the display face is a **variable serif
with real soft/wonk/weight range** (Fraunces-class — verify vendored faces
first, swap only within OFL), and headlines **PROOF as they enter the
viewport** — arriving slightly condensed, light, and wonk-tilted, then
swelling to full weight and roundness like dough rising, one breath, done
(font-variation-settings + transform/opacity only; static full-weight is the
default markup state). Every letterform on the page belongs to the café's own
grammar: the wordmark, the stamp mark, the menu board, the price tags — all
code-drawn, zero minted lettering anywhere.

**The identity mark:** the ampersand of FERN & CRUMB is drawn as a **latte-art
fern rosette** (SVG) — the name's two halves joined by the pour. It recurs as
a small round "cup stamp" section divider (the code-drawn answer to the
sweep's cup-stamp world).

**The morning arc (soft-organic, NOT a scroll clock):** chapters carry a
static warmth grade — first light → full morning → the room at ten — done as
per-chapter background parchment temperature and the mints' own light. No
animated sky, no gauge: ⑯ owns the weather-clock mechanism; this page's one
moving system is the type itself.

**The instrument (small, honest):** a code-drawn **oven timeline** — the
morning bake as a typographic schedule (05:30 first fire · 06:10 sourdough
out · 07:05 croissants · 09:40 second bake) with the rack mint beside it as
the sensory reading. Data-shaped info stays code-drawn (0cr); the
consumable-result rule pairs it with the crumb shot.

**The wink:** in the menu board, the **croissant entry has a bite taken out
of its last letterform** — a clean bite-notch in the final "t", three crumbs
(SVG dots) fallen beneath it. One playful flaw, visible statically, no-JS.

**Casting (§7 + the social-register dial + food corollary, applied
verbatim):** a café is a social purchase — people earn the frame as the
warmth layer, one-two moments, seasoning not cast. (a) THE PAIR: candid
attractive young couple/friends at the window table engaged with EACH OTHER,
flat whites and a shared pastry near in frame, faces present, never zoomed,
never camera-staring. (b) THE KEEPER: one absorbed barista — warm young
female (cafés lead female or warm young male; wave balance after ⑫/⑯ males)
— mid-pour at the machine, plain matte knit (worn-fabric rule; NO apron —
aprons are embroidery magnets). Heroes and food stills keep the object as
protagonist.

## Chapters

1. **HERO** — full-bleed morning-room mint (S1): low sun raking a timber
   table, terracotta cup steaming, croissant on a plain plate, monstera
   shadow falling ACROSS the table echoing a fern frond, street tree soft
   through the glass behind. Code-drawn wordmark FERN & CRUMB proofs in,
   ampersand = the fern rosette. No people (the room before it opens).
2. **THE MORNING BAKE** — the oven-timeline instrument + crumb macro (S2,

   contour map) + the rack lineup (S6) with honest code-drawn names/prices.
3. **THE POUR** — latte fern macro mid-pour (S3); copy on the ritual; the
   stamp-mark divider; frond-shadow motif recurs code-drawn.
4. **THE ROOM** — the pair at the window (S4); urban-nature copy (the street
   tree, the corner's 8am light); plants against glass.
5. **THE BOARD** — the fully code-drawn typographic menu: editorial scale
   jumps, wonk-axis item names, tabular prices, the croissant bite wink.
   The novel-typography centerpiece; zero mint risk by construction.
6. **THE KEEPER** — the barista absorbed (S5); hours + find-us set in the
   menu grammar; CTA honest and small: "The first pour's at seven." + /guide.

## No-JS / reduced-motion

The page is COMPLETE static: default markup is full-weight, fully-proofed
type; the proofing entrance is a JS/IntersectionObserver enhancement over
font-variation-settings, and reduced-motion never runs it. Steam drift is a
CSS loop with a reduced-motion static fallback. The wink, menu, timeline,
and all six mints read identically without JS.

## Decision annotations

- All in-scene text code-drawn → the pseudo-text mint class stays retired;
  no text-precise seat budgeted at all.
- The proofing-type system is THE one animation system; the morning arc is
  static per-chapter grading, deliberately NOT a scroll clock (⑯ owns that
  mechanism; distinctness is structural, not palette-deep).
- Drenched single-colour register refused → reserved for ⑲ (wave
  distinctness); parchment morning world is this site's colour identity.
- People appear exactly twice (the dial: social vertical, warmth layer,
  seasoning not cast); every food-adjacent human scene is the engaged PAIR,
  never cropped hands over plates (food-casting corollary, verbatim).
- The menu board refuses the chalk-font cliché: it is set in the site's own
  display face — the board is the typography exercise, not a texture prop.
- Nature mirrors the subject (§9): frond shadow ↔ latte fern; window tree ↔
  the room's plants; the hero's brightest light answers "café = cramped
  counter queue" dread (§8) with air and morning.

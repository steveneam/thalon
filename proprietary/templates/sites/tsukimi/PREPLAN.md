## Look-first taste notes (Dribbble japanese-restaurant · Pinterest dark-moody-food · motionsites cinematic · Onice + Joji re-glanced)

1. **The two founder poles are a spectrum, and this site lives in the middle.**
   Onice = warm earth-terracotta, wide-tracked serif, editorial white space, a
   press-quote hero, restraint. Joji = drenched single-colour macro panels,
   staccato copy, mono captions, nightlife. The fusion: **drenched colour
   (Joji) delivered with editorial restraint and one Didone serif (Onice)** —
   luxe, not loud; a night, not a nightclub.
2. **Chiaroscuro ingredients are the palette engine (Pinterest).** The strong
   dark-food register is single ingredients glowing as jewels out of near-black
   — a bowl of blueberries, blood oranges, one crimson cut of fish. Steal the
   principle: each course is ONE ingredient, one saturated colour, emerging
   from black. That IS the exceptional-palette exercise — four worlds, one arc.
3. **The moon-portal is the cinematic focal figure (motionsites Dreamcore).**
   A glowing disc in a dark natural scene reads instantly as depth and calm.
   Ours is code-drawn (0cr, and it can move): a real moon that crosses the sky
   on the scroll — the cinematic clock and the nature-frame in one element.
4. **Cinematic = colour grading + one held quote (motionsites Prisma).** The
   restraint move: amid the drenched courses, one full-bleed black chapter with
   a single Didone pull-quote (the chef's line) — silence between colours, the
   way a tasting menu paces itself.
5. **The Japanese vocabulary to steal the PRINCIPLE of, never the pixels
   (Dribbble):** koi line-work, seigaiha wave arcs, the moon disc, the rabbit
   in the moon (tsuki no usagi — mochi-pounding rabbit, the Tsukimi legend).
   All code-drawn as fine line SVG, sparingly — the food carries the colour,

6. **Refused defaults (named):** the red-lantern / cherry-blossom / "authentic
   experience" tourist cliché · the neon-Tokyo cyberpunk skin · stock chopstick
   -over-ramen hero · the dark ANIMATED-BACKGROUND particle hero (motionsites'
   own default — ours is a specific night with a real moon and a real dish, not
   drifting sparkles) · faux-Japanese "brush" display fonts.

## The concept — A TASTING MENU YOU READ BY MOONLIGHT

The page is one night. It opens at moonrise and moves through the courses as
the moon crosses a fixed night sky; each course is a **drenched single-colour
ingredient world** (the exceptional palette), and the moon — a code-drawn disc
with the faint rabbit in its face — tracks overhead on the scroll (the one
animation system; the cinematic clock and the §9 nature-frame in one). Between
the colour worlds sits one held black chapter with a single Didone quote —
the pacing of a real omakase. The palette arcs crimson → amber → green →
violet, the four seasons/phases of the meal, then resolves to a warm lantern
close where the one human moment lives.

**The lunar instrument (code-drawn, 0cr, honest):** a small **moon-phase
marker** in the header — "tonight · waxing gibbous" with a code-drawn phase
disc — ties the fictional menu to the actual sky (the kitchen follows the
moon). A quiet nature-instrument, not a dashboard.

**The wink:** the **rabbit in the moon** (tsuki no usagi) — a faint code-drawn
rabbit silhouette pounding mochi, visible only if you look into the moon disc.
The Tsukimi legend, for regulars. Visible statically in no-JS.

**Nature-vs-dread / §9:** a restaurant's "dread" is pretension and the
unreadable menu; the answer is the moon and the seasons — the page says the
food is just nature at its peak moment, plated. The moon leads; the dishes are
the supporting cast lit by it; people appear once, warm, at the close.

**Casting (the dial, dining = social but food-forward):** the drenched course
worlds are PEOPLE-FREE — the ingredient is the star. ONE human moment at the
close: an absorbed chef at the omakase counter under warm lantern light,
plating, face present but never zoomed (craft-trust, the §7 practitioner
model; the food-casting corollary's caution honoured — a face is present, not
anonymous hands over food). Cool moonlit courses → one warm human close is the
cinematic turn.

## Type & fonts (OFL, vendored at build)

Display: **Bodoni Moda** (variable — high-contrast Didone; the luxe-editorial
drenched register, the Onice serif pole; already vendored elsewhere, verify).
Body: **Instrument Sans** (quiet, readable on dark). Captions / course numbers
/ the lunar marker: **Chivo Mono** (the Joji mono pole). The 月見 / course
kanji (緋琥翠紫) render as Unicode text with system-CJK fallback — a handful of
common glyphs, never a vendored CJK face (too heavy); if a glyph risks
absence, draw it as a small SVG path. One display family only.

## No-JS / reduced-motion

Complete static night: the moon sits at a fixed position per chapter (CSS,
one per section), every course world renders with its drenched background and
mint, the held quote, the phase marker, the rabbit wink, and all six mints
read identically. The moon-crossing is a scroll-linked JS enhancement
(transform/opacity via rAF over a fixed sky); reduced-motion pins the moon and
never animates. Text always rides solid ink/glass plates so AA holds on every
drenched background by construction.

## Decision annotations

- Exceptional-palette is delivered as FOUR drenched single-colour worlds in an
  arc (crimson→amber→green→violet) — an unforgettable colour world, and the
  distinctness mechanism: no portfolio site is a drenched night (⑰ parchment
  morning, ⑱ white catalogue, First Crack warm roastery — all daylight).
- All in-scene text code-drawn → the pseudo-text mint class stays retired; the
  drenched food macros are the lowest-risk class (single subject on black).
- The moon is the ONE animation system and the §9 nature-frame; per-course
  palette is static grading (no second motion system).
- People appear exactly ONCE (the dial: food-forward dining, one warm human
  close); the chef's face is present, never anonymous hands over food.
- The Japanese line-work (koi/seigaiha/rabbit) is code-drawn SVG seasoning,
  sparingly — the food carries the colour, per the refused-cliché note.
- Held-quote chapter = the cinematic pacing move (Prisma), and the palette's
  breath between colours.

How this page was made

Houselights is a fictional demonstration business. Every image on the landing page is AI-generated, minted specifically for this design, and recorded with full provenance (model, prompt, spend, license tier) at generation time.

The idea

The page is built on one conceit: the house lights dimming before a show. A scroll-driven lighting rig takes the whole page from golden hour to show-dark and then brings a warm glow back up for the close — and as the page darkens, the photographs answer: their light sources (chandeliers, the stage spot, the marquee bulb) physically brighten and bloom, the way real lights win the frame as night comes. Typography is the second instrument: a changeable letterboard rides the corner of the viewport and re-letters itself at every act of the page, the way a venue's lobby board names the night.

Method

  • Look first. Studied event/festival and theatre design on Dribbble and Pinterest (look-and-learn only — no reference imagery is ever downloaded into the pipeline): programme-as-typography, editorial serif at poster scale, physical marquee letterboards, blue-hour signage palettes.
  • Design scaffold. The page was storyboarded and then graduated to a final design in a working design surface before any production code, with named slots for every image.
  • Minted imagery. Four scene stills were generated with Soul v2 (Higgsfield); the hero — a marquee that must legibly read HOUSELIGHTS — was generated with Nano Banana Pro, a text-precise image model, after Soul v2 produced garbled lettering in two takes. Rejected takes are kept on record with their reasons. Total image spend for the page: about 3 credits.
  • Code, not credits. The letterboard, the lighting rig, the bloom, and the neon flicker are plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript in one self-contained file — no frameworks, no external hosts, fonts vendored under their SIL Open Font License.

Accessibility & honesty

  • Reduced-motion users get instant letter swaps, a steady sign, and position-mapped lighting only; no autonomous animation.
  • Without JavaScript the full page reads statically, board included.
  • All copy is illustrative; no real venue, statistics, or events are described.

Iteration passes

  • Pass 1 — fault-hunt: fixed a broken stats row (labels collapsing), removed a duplicated still (the hands image appeared twice), moved the board section to full width. Ambition: committed to the re-lettering corner board instead of a static exhibit.
  • Pass 2 — fault-hunt: hero text-shadow legibility over the marquee photo; blank-tile rhythm on the board. Ambition: the continuous lighting rig (dimmer + warm restore) replaced static section backgrounds as the page's arc.
  • Pass 3 — fault-hunt: bloom was too subtle against the dimmer (operator review); the stills now brighten directly while the page darkens. Ambition: the closing sign ignites toward curtain and one letter carries a slow old-neon flicker.

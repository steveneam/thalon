How this page was made

Ridge & Valley Roofing is a fictional demonstration business. Every claim on the landing page is illustrative, every photograph is AI-generated, and this note explains the method honestly.

The design

The page is built like a roof under weather. Its brutalism is material, not decorative: every chapter opens under a load-bearing ridge rule, columns split along valley channels, the specs are stamped into plates, and there is not a single rounded corner anywhere — the only fully square site in this portfolio, on purpose.

One scroll clock runs the whole page: the sky layer darkens from clear zinc noon to a supercell at the storm-test chapter, then resolves to washed evening gold at the close. A code-drawn storm gauge (rain, gusts, gutter load) climbs on the same clock beside a code-drawn roof cross-section that visibly sheds the storm — the expert instrument and its lay twin on one clock. Content rides paper plates stacked over the weather, so text never sits on the changing sky and contrast holds at every storm stage by construction. Without JavaScript, or under reduced motion, the storm chapter carries its weather statically — peak gauge readings, rain as still texture — and the rest of the page is the complete, calm essay.

The wink: at the storm's peak, one card wears a blue emergency tarp over its corner. Temporary fix — book the permanent one.

The imagery

All six photographs were generated with Higgsfield Soul 2.0 at 2K and pinned with full provenance manifests (model, prompt, credits, licence tier). Six slots, six first-take keepers, 0.72 credits total — every in-scene word on this site is drawn in code, which structurally removes the class of AI-imagery failure where models invent garbled lettering on printable surfaces.

The type

Barlow Condensed 700 for the poster type (the DIN/roadside-signage tradition — the trade's own letterforms), Barlow 400/600 for text (one family, brutalist discipline), Fragment Mono for spec plates and the gauge. All SIL Open Font Licence, vendored locally — this page makes zero external requests.

What the build pass caught

  • Chapter text originally sat straight on the changing sky — at storm peak it would have failed contrast. The paper-plate structure was added so AA holds at every scroll position by construction.
  • The tarp wink covered its own emergency copy on phones — the card now reserves the tarp's corner and the tarp shrinks on small screens.
  • Cross-section labels overflowed the drawing's edge on both instruments; re-anchored with leader lines.
  • The giant display type is cropped off the page edge deliberately (the brutalist signature); horizontal overflow is clipped so the crop never becomes a scrollbar.

Method

Designed and built by the Thalon engine's template factory: a look-first reference sweep (principles only — no images are ever downloaded into the pipeline), a written pre-plan (concept, scroll mechanism, per-slot mint briefs and pre-armed risks — recorded before any code or imagery), minted originals pinned at generation time, then at least three iteration passes hunting faults and pushing ambition. Specs shown are illustrative; a real roofing client would supply real numbers, and the engine would keep them honest.

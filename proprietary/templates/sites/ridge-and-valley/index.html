Chapter — anatomyR&V · 01

A roof is a system, not a surface

What fails in a storm is almost never the sheet you can see. It is the ridge capping that was face-fixed instead of clipped, the sarking that stops short of the valley, the gutter that was never sized for the catchment above it. A re-roof replaces the system.

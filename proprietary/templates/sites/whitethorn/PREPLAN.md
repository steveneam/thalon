### A draw-rule amendment this arc forces, stated rather than hidden

The meta-prompt's draw rule says *"no two sites in a wave share a primary
axis."* This arc's three sites are, by founder direction, **an animation
family** — all three exist for their instrument, so all three want the
animation axis. The rule cannot hold literally here. **The distinctness
burden moves to the secondary axis, the palette, the type and the
instrument's own grammar**, and each site records its pair as
portfolio-unique instead. Recorded so the next re-charter can accept or
reject it rather than inherit it silently.

## Look-first taste notes (Dribbble "veterinary website" · motionsites.ai)

1. **The vet genre default is one page, drawn twenty times.** Across ~20
   shots: mint/teal + white (or teal + orange), a golden retriever staring
   down the lens, a scrubs-and-stethoscope doctor with folded arms, stat
   counters (`20 years / 3,000 per year / 6,500 animals`), icon service
   card-grids, paw-print scatter, and one of three headlines — *"Trusted
   Partner in Veterinary Care" · "Loving Care For Your Furry Family
   Members" · "Professional Veterinary Care You Can Trust."* **All refused,
   by name.** Teal is refused outright: it is the single strongest tell.
2. **Refuse Wagtail too.** ⑨ already holds soft-organic + paw marginalia +
   cream paper for dog care. A second dog page in that register would be the
   portfolio repeating itself in a wave meant to add options. Whitethorn is
   **cooler, older, and instrument-led** — a country practice, not a nursery.
3. **motionsites.ai read double-edged, as the meta-prompt instructs.** Its
   own site IS the current AI-landing default it warns about: near-black
   ground, glowing gradient headline, gradient CTA pill. Refused. The
   gallery's genuinely strong entries were the *light* ones — a wellness
   page running a huge serif word across a photographic natural scene, the
   image passing between the letterforms.
4. **The principle worth stealing (never the pixels): type that INTERLOCKS
   with the photographic subject.** Depth from layering rather than from
   parallax tricks — the display line sits behind the dog's shoulder and in
   front of the field. One move, used once, in the hero.
5. **A recovery is the only story a vet page can tell that a brochure
   cannot.** Nothing in the sweep showed an outcome over time. Every page
   asserted care; none demonstrated it. That gap is this site's whole
   concept.

## The concept — the page is A WALK THAT GETS BETTER

The founder's banked idea was *"an animated dog beside the scroll/leash line
whose state changes as you scroll"* (s55). Moved to a vet, the state stops
being a schedule and becomes **a recovery**: a drawn dog walks a path beside
the reader, and **its gait is the argument**. It arrives with a three-beat
limp and leaves at a run. The page's central claim is never asserted in a
sentence — it is demonstrated in the instrument, which is the representation
ladder applied at full strength.

**One clock, two readings (instrument + lay twin, s60 rule):** the same
scroll drives a clinical **gait readout** (day · lameness score 0–5 · stride
symmetry % · weight-bearing %) and the **dog visibly walking better**. Vets
genuinely score lameness this way, so the instrument is honest rather than
decorative. The numbers earn the expert's trust; the walk carries everyone
else. The twin tracks the instrument exactly — reduced-motion and no-JS get
the complete sequence as stills with the same numbers.

**Why the dog is DRAWN and the hero is MINTED.** The hero register rule
(s37) forbids a code-drawn hero on a premium vertical, so photography leads.
But the instrument earns its drawing: a **gait study is a drawing in real
veterinary practice** — being a diagram IS the story, which is exactly the
inner-scene exemption the rule names. It is also 0cr and it can respond to
the visitor, which the meta-prompt prefers for data-shaped information.

**The wink (§casting 6):** at Day 84 and only there, the dog turns and looks
out at the reader. One camera-meet, at the end, on a page that otherwise
obeys the never-stare rule absolutely — the rule broken once, on purpose, at
the moment it means recovery. Visible statically in no-JS.

**Nature-vs-dread (§8):** this vertical's mental image is the stainless
table, the cage and the corridor of chairs. The page's answer is a section
called **"There is no waiting room"** — a walled garden with a gate open onto
a green, and it carries the brightest image on the page. If glazing appears
anywhere: ONE large pane, never a mullioned grid (s63 corollary — bars read
as a cage and reintroduce the exact dread the shot exists to dispel).

**Casting (§7, founder's own note in the arc spec):** a vet is both a
professional/serious vertical and a warmth vertical, so the archetype shifts
**older and calmer** than Wagtail's. Cast: an **older woman with visible
experience** — silver hair, plain matte knit, hands on the animal. That reads
as both medical wisdom and animal warmth, which is the pair this vertical
actually needs. *This is a lead judgement call where §7 gives two answers;
it is a one-line reversal if the founder reads it differently.*

## The mock's finding (why the claude-design stage earned its place)

The mock stacked the instrument above six chapters — and rendered, that is
plainly wrong: **the instrument scrolls away and leaves the reader six
paragraphs with no dog in them.** The gait has nothing to react to.

**The fix, and it is now the build's spine: the instrument PINS and the
chapters drive it.** The gait study is a sticky stage; chapter text scrolls
past it; scroll progress through the chapter column is the clock that sets
the gait, the day and the readouts. That is a structural decision the mock
surfaced and the prose plan did not.

## Chapters

1. **HERO** — minted: a collie mid-stride on a worn path, open country,
   morning; the practitioner crouched at the path edge. Display line
   interlocks with the photograph (taste note 4). Neither subject meets the
   lens. Headline: *"Getting them back to the long way round."*
2. **THE GAIT STUDY** — the instrument. Sticky stage + six driving chapters
   (Day 0 the limp · Day 1 the exam · Day 6 the images · Day 12 the repair ·
   Day 40 rehab · Day 84 the long way round). The wink lands at 84.
3. **WHAT WE DO** — six services, each one honest sentence, no icons and no
   counters. Deliberately the quietest section: after the instrument, a
   plain readable list is a relief rather than a let-down.
4. **THE YARD** — the dread-counter, brightest mint on the page.
5. **THE SAME VET, EACH TIME** — one absorbed practitioner, hedgerow behind;
   nature primary, person secondary.
6. **VISITING + close** — hours, the lane, booking as the only action; the
   closing mint is the causal pair to the hero (the same dog, running).
   Footer → `/guide`.

## Type & fonts (portfolio-new, OFL, vendored at build)

**Petrona** (variable serif — warm, bookish, slightly narrow) + **Familjen
Grotesk** (humanist grotesk body) + **DM Mono** (instrument readings only).
Checked against every face already vendored across the portfolio: none of

Google Fonts links; the built site must not).

## No-JS / reduced-motion

The recovery **resolves rather than disappears**. Without JS the sticky stage
becomes a static sequence: all six chapters visible, each with its own gait
still and its own complete readout row, the wink frame present at Day 84.
Reduced-motion gets the same. The scroll clock is a progressive enhancement
over `transform`/`opacity` only; the default markup state is the complete
static essay.

## Decision annotations

- **The gait is the only animation on the page.** One clock. A page about
  measured movement cannot itself be busy.
- **Haw red is instrument-only.** Every live reading is haw; nothing
  decorative borrows it. The accent means "this number is being measured."
- **Teal is banned on this site**, as the genre's strongest tell.
- **The lameness scale is real (0–5) and used correctly** — an invented
  scale would be the honesty failure this vertical is most exposed to.
- **Bracken is a composite case at a business that does not exist**, said on
  the page itself under the instrument (not only in `/guide`) — the claim
  sits where the claim is made.
- **No testimonials, no counters, no urgency.** The instrument is the proof,
  which is the entire reason it exists.
- **Text never rides the moving stage** — chapter copy sits on solid paper,
  so contrast holds at every frame by construction (the ⑯ paper-plates
  lesson, carried).

How this page was made

Hue & Cry is a fictional demonstration business. The salon, the address, the prices and the correction notes are illustrative copy for a demo — no real service exists, and no real client's hair is depicted. All imagery is AI-generated.

Design

A look-first study came before any code: salon sites (to learn the genre defaults and avoid them — cream-serif "Elevate Your Look" elegance, dark-gold "premium studio" cards), "colorful" landing pages (which default to purple-blue gradient mush — the exact opposite of a committed palette), and real hair-dye shade cards and swatch books, which is where the design actually comes from.

The page is built like a colourist's shade chart: five chapters, each a pigment world mixed tone-on-tone from a single hue family — copper 7.44, honey 8.3, rose 9.26, violet 5.20, blue-black 1.1 — numbered the way colourists actually number tint (level.tone). With JavaScript, the paper itself takes the dye: a fixed background layer cross-fades between chapter pigments as you scroll, mixed in the OKLab colour space (hand-rolled, ~25 lines, no libraries). The shade mixer is a toy level/tone instrument that mixes its swatch in oklch() locally — nothing you touch on this page leaves the page.

Type: Bodoni Moda (display), Schibsted Grotesk (body) and Chivo Mono (the formula register) — all SIL Open Font License, vendored locally with their licenses. Single self-contained file, no frameworks, no external requests; reduced-motion gets hard chapter boundaries with everything pre-developed, and the full content works without JavaScript.

Imagery

Five photographs were generated through the Higgsfield API on a paid license tier; each original is pinned in a content-addressed store with full provenance (model, prompt, credits, license tier). The model named is the model that actually ran. All five were first-take keepers — no rejects this build.

Iteration passes

  • Pass 1 (fault-hunt): full browser walk, desktop and 390 px, scroll-dye blending checked at every chapter boundary. Caught: the rotated hero caption clipping at the mobile viewport edge (it now sits below the image on small screens); a static/JS mismatch in the mixer's level readout; a vendored italic font face nothing used (removed); a chip-tracking bug that could snap the rail back to copper around the chart section; aria-current hygiene (set or removed, never "false").
  • Pass 2 (ambition-push + colour audit): added the shade-book tabs — punched swatch cards at each chapter head — and taught the wordmark to take the active chapter's accent as you scroll. Measured every ink-on-paper pairing (all ≥7:1); the hero's italic accent sat at 2.81:1 against the 3:1 large-text floor, so the copper accent was darkened from #B33D0E to #9C3108 (3.5:1) here and on this page.
  • Pass 3 (fault-hunt): reduced-motion path exercised via an injected media stub (hard chapter colours, chip tracking intact, no develop filter, everything visible); no-JS state exercised by stripping the gate classes (dye layer hidden, all five chapters paint their own paper, mixer shows its static default, all content and prices readable); copy honesty re-read against the imagery ("six chairs" became "a small room" — the night frame shows three); console clean; every network request stays on the page's own origin.

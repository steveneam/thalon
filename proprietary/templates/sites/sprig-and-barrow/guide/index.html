How this page was made

Sprig & Barrow is a fictional demonstration business. There is no stall, no Barrow Lane, and no figs — this page exists to show what our content factory can design and build for a real greengrocer. All photographic imagery is AI-generated (models listed below), minted on a paid license tier and recorded with full provenance at mint time. Nothing was scraped or stock-sourced.

The design

Two assigned axes drive the page. The primary is physics & interaction: the day's produce rests on a chalk shelf in the hero and falls past you as you scroll — the day selling through. The fall is deliberately not a physics engine: every position and rotation is a pure function of scroll progress with per-item variance, so it is deterministic, cheap, and reversible (scroll back up and the day un-happens). The kilo-scale is the same idea at interaction scale — pick produce, the needle swings, the total reads out.

The secondary axis is exceptional palette: each section takes its colour world from one produce family — market-paper cream before dawn, tomato vermilion for the crate, citrus amber for the scale, chard green for the hands, plum dusk for closing. The photography was briefed to the same palette (the citrus macro is the palette section's source).

If you're reading with JavaScript off, or with reduced motion set: the produce simply rests on its shelf, and every word and price on the page is still there. The falling is seasoning, not the meal.

Build notes

  • One self-contained HTML file. No frameworks, no external requests; the two typefaces (Fraunces, Work Sans — both SIL Open Font License) are vendored beside the page.
  • Review passes: three full passes, each hunting faults top-to-bottom and pushing one ambition item. Pass 1 caught a chalk-arrow colliding with its own note, the fig row wearing a plum icon (a fig got drawn), a 4-pixel mobile overflow from the wonky carrot's label, and falling produce passing behind translucent panels — the fall was moved to the front plane, past the page instead of under it. Pass 2 walked every mobile section and the scale's states, and fixed an hour-carry rounding bug in the new ambition item: the day clock, which reads scroll progress back as market time (5:40 am at the top of the page, six in the evening at the bottom). Pass 3 was the final sweep — both breakpoints, console clean, keyboard focus, and the squint test against the rest of the portfolio.
  • Honesty rules: no fake urgency, no invented testimonials, no real address or phone. The ledger's quantities are illustrative and say so here.

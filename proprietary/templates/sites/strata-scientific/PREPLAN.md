## Look-first taste notes (Dribbble lab-branding · Dribbble swiss-editorial · Pinterest polarized-light micrographs · motionsites.ai)

1. **The tabbed spec-sheet SYSTEM is the vertical's native grammar** — the
   strongest sweep find is the colour-tabbed science brochure family (one
   tab per product line, figure + table per sheet). Steal the system, not
   the sheets: each product family on our page is one "sheet" with its
   figure, its table, its tab colour drawn from its own micrograph.
2. **Print furniture is the register** (the SPace®/K24 poster class):
   folios, registration marks, index numbers, rules-and-columns, captions
   set flush under figures. The page wears the apparatus of PRINT honestly —
   and the wink lives there (below).
3. **Micrograph birefringence is the colour engine, BOUNDED.** The
   polarized-light crystal tiles are spectacular saturated rainbows on

   captioned journal FIGURES in white editorial space, each with a
   code-drawn scale bar. Distinctness is structural: white field, colour
   only inside the plates.
4. **Letterform-borrows-the-instrument transfers but is REFUSED here** —
   the lab-logo grid (microscope-as-C, test-tube-P) proves the ⑰ principle
   generalizes; a B2B distributor's restraint says no logo-play — the
   identity is the setting itself (serif + mono + rules).
5. **Refused defaults (named):** the dark neuron-stock "Understanding the

   minimal-to-absent, so: none) · icon feature grids · glassmorphism
   dashboards wearing "science" as a skin.
6. **motionsites.ai pass:** nothing lab-adjacent; the transferable principle
   (motion lives IN the subject, never behind it) lands here as the focus
   rack — the figure itself resolves, no ambient animation exists.

## The concept — CATALOGUE No. 7: a print catalog built as a SLIDE TRAY

The page reads as a beautifully set print catalog (masthead, contents line,
folios, rules) whose figure system is **prepared microscope slides**: each
product family is one SLIDE — a white editorial field with a frosted label
edge (code-drawn mono: family name, batch format, storage temp), a specimen
window holding the family's micrograph plate (the bounded colour), an honest
scale bar, and the spec TABLE set beneath in catalog columns. *Strata* is
enacted, not stated: the chapters stack like strata in a core sample, each
with its index tab.

**The instrument (data-instrument secondary, with its lay twin):** a
code-drawn **certificate of analysis** for one exemplar batch — assay %,
conductivity, endotoxin line — beside a code-drawn **HPLC purity trace**
that draws itself once when it enters view. The certificate earns the
buyer's trust; the micrograph beauty above it carries everyone else. One
batch, two readings.

**The focus rack (the one moving system):** figures enter ~8px blurred and
rack sharp over one breath (filter: blur + opacity only — compositor-cheap);
reduced-motion and no-JS get sharp figures always (default markup state).

**The wink:** the page carries four corner registration marks like a print
proof; the bottom-right one is printed a few degrees OFF-REGISTER, with a
tiny mono marginalia note beside it — "checked twice." Visible statically.

**Casting (the dial, precision/B2B reading):** ZERO people on the page.
The §8 counter-move is light and air instead — the hero and one inner band
are sun, glass, and greenery beyond the pane; the lab reads like a place
with windows, not a basement.

## Decision annotations

- Colour discipline is the distinctness mechanism: white/ink/blue field,
  saturation ONLY inside bounded figure plates → cannot be confused with
  ⑲'s drenched worlds or ⑰'s parchment morning.
- All in-scene text code-drawn (labels, scale bars, certificate, tables) —
  the pseudo-text mint class stays retired; micrograph slots are abstract
  continuous fields, the lowest-risk mint class in the portfolio.
- Zero people is a POSITIVE casting decision (the dial's B2B reading), not
  an omission; §8's dread-counter is carried entirely by light/air/green.
- The certificate + trace are the data-instrument quota; no dashboard-decor
  anywhere else — editorial-print leads, instrument supports.
- Vance & Alder (⑦) owns legal-document grammar; distinctness from it is
  structural: ⑦ is a dossier you read down a spine, this is a CATALOG of
  slide-figures with tables — different print species, and the micrograph
  colour engine has no ⑦ counterpart.

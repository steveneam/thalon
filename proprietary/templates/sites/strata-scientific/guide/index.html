How this page was made

Strata Scientific is a fictional demonstration business. This page is a portfolio template — it shows how a B2B science-supply catalog can be designed, written, and illustrated by an AI-driven content engine. No product, lot number, certificate, or address on it is real.

The design

The page is built as a print catalog — Catalogue No. 7 — whose figure system is prepared microscope slides. Each product family is one slide: a frosted label edge, a specimen window holding that family's micrograph, a scale bar, and a spec table in catalog columns. The masthead, contents line, folios, registration marks, the certificate of analysis, the HPLC purity trace, every label, scale bar and table are HTML and SVG — not images. The one moving element is the "focus rack": figures enter softly blurred and rack into focus as you scroll, the way a microscope finds its plane. The purity trace draws itself once. Without JavaScript, or with reduced motion enabled, everything is simply sharp and complete.

A note on the numbers

The certificate of analysis and the HPLC trace are illustrative — plausible values for a demonstration, not a real release document. They exist to show the shape of what a supplier's paperwork looks like, not to certify anything.

The imagery — all AI-generated

The six photographs were generated with the Higgsfield Soul 2.0 model. No stock, no scraped references. The three abstract figures (a polarized-light crystal field, a filtration-membrane matrix, a stained-cell field) are generated interpretations of scientific imagery, not real micrographs of any real sample. Honest disclosures on the two real-object photographs: the hero glassware shot and the consumables shot were each re-generated once because the model had invented illegible label text on the vessels — we compose invented text out rather than ship it. One small residual artifact (a molded mark on one flask) was locally blurred before archiving.

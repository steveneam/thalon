How this page was made

Orchard House is a fictional demonstration business. Every image on the landing page is AI-generated, minted specifically for this design, and recorded with full provenance (model, prompt, spend, license tier) at generation time.

The idea

One tree, one year. The page is a single scroll through the orchard’s four seasons: the same painted hillside — the same apple tree right of center, the same farmhouse with its lit window — crosses from autumn into winter, spring and summer as you read. The season is never a slideshow of four different pictures; it is one continuous place changing state, which is also how the fictional kitchen’s menu works: four chapters, four cards, one orchard.

The falling things are not part of the paintings. Autumn leaves tumble and settle, winter snow drifts and rests, spring blossom pops in a single burst at the turn and then floats, and summer’s gold motes rise in the evening light — all drawn in code on a canvas over the scene, each season with its own particle shape, physics and pace, and a small “gust at the turn” as one season hands over to the next. With reduced motion enabled the particles are gone and the seasons swap cleanly; without JavaScript the four chapters simply show their scenes as plain images.

The imagery

The four backdrops are an AI-minted painterly series. The autumn master was generated first (Higgsfield, Nano Banana 2 — the vendor quietly routes “pro” requests to this seat on the current plan; the request and the model that actually ran are both in the provenance record). The other three seasons were then generated as edits of that master (Nano Banana Flash, image-to-image), each holding the same camera, composition and brushwork while repainting the season: bare branches and snow, full blossom over new grass, deep summer leaf with red apples. All four were keepers on the first take — 8 credits for the whole series — and every one was pinned to the content-addressed store with its full provenance manifest at mint time.

The type

Vollkorn — the “wholegrain” old-style serif — carries the whole page in two registers: the variable roman and italic for prose, dishes and notes, and Vollkorn SC’s letterpress small caps for the wordmark, season heads and labels. One family, four seasons. Both faces are open-licensed (SIL OFL) and served from this site’s own directory; the page makes no external requests at all.

Honesty notes

  • Orchard House, its menu, its address and its people are fictional; the page exists to demonstrate what this template factory can build.
  • All landscape imagery is AI-generated, as described above — nothing was photographed, and nothing was taken from anyone else’s work.
  • Prices and dishes are plausible fictions, not offers.
  • The booking address is a reserved example domain that cannot receive mail.

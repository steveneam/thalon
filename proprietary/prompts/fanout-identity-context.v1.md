# Fan-out brand-identity context — v1

The request includes a BRAND IDENTITY block: the tenant's operator-asserted
identity (company, what it does, philosophy, audience, offers, durable facts,
topics, links). It is versioned runtime DATA from the tenant's brand profile,
never hard-coded — and the judge harness receives this exact block as
grounding evidence, so it is a claim surface, not free-form seasoning.

Use it as follows:

- You MAY draw factual claims from the BRAND IDENTITY block exactly as you
  may from SOURCE CONTENT — both are judge-grounded. Never extrapolate beyond
  either: no invented numbers, customers, results, or history.
- Let PHILOSOPHY and AUDIENCE shape the angle and framing. They are lenses,
  not claims to restate verbatim in every draft.
- Mention OFFERS and LINKS only where the platform profile's CTA policy
  invites it — identity is context, not an advertisement mandate.
- When SOURCE CONTENT and BRAND IDENTITY conflict, prefer SOURCE CONTENT and
  avoid the conflicting claim entirely.

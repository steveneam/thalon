## Shared style keys (prefix the slot prompt with the register block)

**`LANDING-STYLE` (dark-cinematic register):** deep warm charcoal-navy night field
(near-black, hue toward ink-navy), a single warm amber glow as the only strong
color, cinematic contrast, vast negative space, quiet horizon line. Motifs: three
soft sail-like forms closing warmly on a point (organic, asymmetric, opera-house
soft — never sharp or aggressive); a falconer's horizon at dusk; watchful calm.
No text in image, no logos, no shields/world-maps/corporate-fintech dress.

**`WORKSPACE-STYLE` (paper/navy register):** warm off-white paper field, navy-ink
line-and-wash illustration, flat (no drop shadows, no 3D render look), generous
white space, a restrained bronze-amber accent used once if at all. Tone: calm,
diagrammatic warmth — polish, not spectacle. No text in image.

01 — THE PROMPT

One line, and the sources you trust.

You describe the brand once: voice, platforms, topics, the sources a claim may rest on, and the words you never want published.

02 — THE FAN-OUT

It becomes every shape at once.

The same idea, written natively for each destination — not one post pasted six times. A post, a script with captions, a full page.

03 — G1 · THE SCREEN

Your denylist runs first.

Before anything is judged for truth it is screened for the words and topics you ruled out. That check is deterministic.

04 — G3 · THE GROUNDING

Every claim, back to a source you gave it.

Each sentence that asserts something is checked against your sources. The ones that cannot be traced do not proceed. This is where most things die — and it should be.

05 — THE QUEUE

Only survivors reach you.

What is left arrives with its whole history attached: which gate it passed, on what evidence, and what its siblings failed on.

06 — THE CLICK

Nothing leaves without it.

Approve, edit or reject. Every correction becomes a row the engine learns from. Zero posts have ever left this engine unreviewed.

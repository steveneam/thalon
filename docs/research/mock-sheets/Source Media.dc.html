The one case cover-crop gets badly wrong
Cover-cropping a 9:16 Short into a 1.6 box discards ~65% of the frame height and reliably cuts the subject's head off. A 16:9 poster only loses ~10% of its width — inside the central-80% safe area creators already design for, so landscape stays cropped.
